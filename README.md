## About Me

I'm a **software developer** and **B.Sc. Informatics graduate of Ionian University**, with interests spanning full-stack engineering, cybersecurity, identity systems, data platforms, and academic research. I enjoy taking technically demanding ideas from an initial concept to a working, maintainable application.

- **Building:** **IonWings**, a flight-data platform covering four airports in the Ionian region.
- **Leading:** Conference organization and technical infrastructure as **Head Organizer & IT Coordinator of [IUSCI](https://iusci.di.ionio.gr/?lang=en)**.
- **Researching:** 5G network security, cyber-range environments, information privacy, and language technologies.
- **Previously:** Cybersecurity internship at **PDMFC**, Lisbon, involving Python automation, identity management, Apereo CAS, and Active Directory.
- **Academic path:** B.Sc. in Informatics and admission to the M.Sc. in **Digital Applications and Innovation**, Ionian University.

> **Areas of interest:** Secure software architecture · Full-stack platforms · Automation and data engineering · Network security · Open-source collaboration

## Selected Work

### 01 · IonWings <img alt="Project type: Aviation platform" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Aviation-Data%20platform-1475BD?style=flat-square" />

A flight-information and operational data platform designed for **Corfu (CFU)**, **Aktion (PVK)**, **Kefalonia (EFL)**, and **Zakynthos (ZTH)**.

- Flight arrivals and departures, structured data ingestion, synchronization, and normalization.
- Data consistency workflows, airport information, and administration tools.
- **Stack:** `Laravel` `PHP` `MySQL` `Python` `GitHub Actions`

### 02 · [IUSCI - Ionian University Student Conference in Informatics](https://iusci.di.ionio.gr/?lang=en) <img alt="Project type: Conference systems" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Academic-Conference%20systems-1475BD?style=flat-square" />

Organization and development of digital infrastructure supporting the **2025 and 2026** editions of the conference.

- Registration and participant services, attendance check-in, and certificate workflows.
- Committee coordination, permissions, reporting, and operational tools.
- **Role:** Head Organizer & IT Coordinator
- **Stack:** `PHP` `MySQL` `JavaScript` `Bootstrap` `WordPress`

### 03 · 5G CyberRange <img alt="Project type: Cybersecurity research" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Cybersecurity-Research-1475BD?style=flat-square" />

My undergraduate thesis explored cybersecurity training, experimentation, and attack mitigation in simulated **5G network environments**.

- Containerized 5G core and radio access network environments.
- Network traffic analysis, replay-based experiments, and security scenarios.
- **Stack:** `Open5GS` `UERANSIM` `Docker` `Wireshark` `tcpdump` `5Greplay`

### 04 · [Desperate John - The Mission of Informatics](https://john.skokotos.com/) <img alt="Project type: Privacy education" src="https://img.shields.io/badge/Education-Privacy-1475BD?style=flat-square" />

An educational escape-room initiative exploring **digital privacy and personal data protection**. I contributed to its web presence and associated research activities.
